Our Mission

72% of people know they should eat healthier but lack real-time, personalized guidance at the moment of food decision. We exist to solve that.

NutriSmart AI combines cutting-edge generative AI with real-time search and maps grounding to deliver instant, trustworthy nutrition advice — whether you're at home cooking, shopping at the grocery store, or choosing a restaurant.

Our Approach

AI-First: Every interaction is powered by Google Gemini 2.0 Flash with 1M token context windows.

Grounded in Reality: Search grounding ensures nutrition data is accurate and up-to-date, not hallucinated.

Voice-Native: The Gemini Live API enables real-time voice conversations for hands-free meal logging.

Privacy-Conscious: No API keys in source code. All secrets managed via environment variables and backend proxies.
